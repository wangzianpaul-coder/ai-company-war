## Architecture Boundary

- `MarketState` is a typed RefCounted that explicitly owns the two fixed slots; it does not use a Dictionary, generic registry, Resource or Node. Its zero/default state is valid and inert.
- Stored integer fields cover the two workloads, Consumer service-allocation bps, two player shares, two addressable monthly-revenue pools, two full-service growth rates, two unmet penalties, two current served values, two current market revenues and four cumulative served/unmet totals. All inputs are copied and exposed only through typed getters.
- Consumer service allocation is in `[0,10000]`; Developer/API is derived as `10000-consumer`. Each market's outside share is derived as `10000-player_share`; the two independent player shares do not sum with each other.
- Active market workloads are non-negative, checked-add to the Compute inference workload, and the configured service split must reproduce the two workloads at full service. Market workloads are bounded so conversion to basis points cannot overflow.
- ComputeState owns the single canonical derived served/unmet-per-month formula. ComputeSystem and MarketSystem consume it; neither Application nor UI recalculates capacity.
- Consumer served is the checked floor of actual inference served times Consumer service-allocation bps; Developer/API receives the exact remainder. Each value is capped by its own workload, and no capacity is banked.
- Full service adds the configured growth bps. Any shortage subtracts `unmet × penalty`; the actual signed change is clamped to `[0,10000]` and the post-clamp delta is emitted.
- Market revenue is the checked floor of `addressable revenue × player share / 10000`, then the checked floor of that value times the service-fulfillment bps. Division of non-negative integers always rounds down. Use quotient/remainder decomposition; never perform an unchecked wide product.
- Before settlement, stored served/share/revenue values must be internally consistent. The two old market revenues must checked-add and may not exceed Company monthly revenue. The non-market remainder is preserved, so Project completion revenue is never overwritten or counted twice.
- MarketSystem computes and validates both markets, Company monthly revenue and all contributions before one atomic commit. Failure returns the existing typed ErrorCode with empty effects and no mutation.
- Exact monthly order becomes `Project → Compute → Finance → Market → Clock` ×3. Market revenue therefore affects the next month's Finance. Any failure discards the complete working quarter and all candidate effects.
- Market effect order is Consumer served, optional unmet, optional share delta, optional market-revenue delta; then Developer/API in the same order. Zero changes are omitted. Revenue contributions sum exactly to the Company market-revenue change.
- Append `BASIS_POINTS` to the existing Effect unit enum; never reorder prior values. Reuse `COMPUTE_UNIT_MONTHS` and `CENTS` for demand/revenue effects.
- GameSession still accepts exactly three concrete command scripts. It aggregates Market explanations only from committed ordered contributions; no new dispatch branch is allowed.
- DashboardViewModel stays primitive-only. Dashboard depends only on GameSession, ViewModel, the three accepted commands and UI types; it never imports Market/Compute State/System/Engine/Effect or performs market arithmetic.
- Main remains the Composition Root. Simulation gains no Node/UI/IO/time/frame/random/Persistence dependency.

## Deterministic Golden Rules

- Initial Consumer: workload 30, service allocation 6,000 bps, share 3,000 bps, pool 100,000 cents, growth 30 bps, penalty 10 bps/unmet, current served 30, current revenue 30,000 cents.
- Initial Developer/API: workload 20, derived allocation 4,000 bps, share 2,000 bps, pool 450,000 cents, growth 10 bps, penalty 25 bps/unmet, current served 20, current revenue 90,000 cents.
- Default Compute serves 50/month: Consumer 30 and Developer/API 20. Three months produce the exact default values in Player-visible Outcome.
- Training 70 serves 20/month: Consumer 12 and Developer/API 8. Consumer loses 180 bps/month; Developer/API loses 300 bps/month. Revenue uses each month's post-change share and 4,000-bps service fulfillment.
- Initial market revenue is exactly the existing Company 120,000 cents/month. Project completion remains the separate 30,000-cent non-market component.
- No RNG, hash, frame time, directory order or float participates in settlement.
